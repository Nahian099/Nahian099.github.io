Research

Research projects

Doctoral research at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, 2022 to 2026.

Preprint · under review at PLOS Computational Biology

X-Admix: multimodal cross-attention for genomic prediction

A deep learning framework that fuses genotype, local ancestry, and social drivers of health through pairwise cross-attention and a learned softmax-gated fusion layer. It captures conditional interactions across data types that additive models miss, and it reached AUC 0.771 where classical baselines stayed near chance.

Read the preprint
Part of X-Admix

Interpretability and ablation analysis

An attribution system that layers feature ablation, gradient-based attribution, and SHAP with a leave-one-component-out protocol. A two-tier consensus strategy ranks cross-modal interactions by reproducibility across folds, separating a stable core from a larger discovery pool.

Published · Scientific Reports 2025

Ancestry-aware asthma risk and treatment response

Ensemble and stacked models integrating genotype and local ancestry in a pediatric asthma cohort (248 patients, about 5 million features). Feature selection combined Lasso, RFE, and Elastic Net with expert priors, and the stacked model beat unimodal baselines by 5 AUC points.

Read the paper
Ongoing · unpublished

Language models for genomic variants

A comparison of four ways to encode variant data for pretrained language models under a fixed token budget. Compressed encodings outperform verbose prose (AUC 0.71 vs. 0.63), and an embedding-injection scheme extends to unseen variants without adding parameters.

Mentoring and technical leadership

I mentored a junior Ph.D. student through a study of social determinants in asthma that was published at IEEE SIEDS 2025, and I advised a student team building named entity recognition models that extract sentencing components from federal court records.
